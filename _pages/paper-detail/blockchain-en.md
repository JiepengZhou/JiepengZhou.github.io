## Overview

This project studies digital intellectual-property protection in an alliance-blockchain environment. It connects copyright registration, provenance records, and platform-level verification in a practical end-to-end system.

## Motivation

Digital works are easy to reproduce and redistribute, while conventional ownership records may be fragmented across platforms. Copyright protection benefits from a shared record that is auditable, difficult to alter, and accessible to authorized participants.

## System Design

- **Copyright registration:** bind a digital work to its ownership and submission metadata.
- **Tamper-resistant records:** store critical provenance events on an alliance blockchain.
- **Lifecycle traceability:** preserve a verifiable history of registration, transfer, and authorization events.
- **Platform implementation:** expose the workflow through a usable application layer rather than treating the blockchain as an isolated prototype.

## Why an Alliance Blockchain

An alliance chain provides controlled participation and governance while retaining shared verification across organizations. This makes it better suited to institutional copyright workflows than a fully public or centrally managed ledger.

## Conclusion

The project demonstrates how distributed records and a practical platform can be combined to improve provenance, accountability, and verification for digital IP.
