## Key Contributions

- Designed and constructed **100+ high-quality task samples** requiring cross-page information aggregation and dynamic multi-hop reasoning.
- Evaluated leading proprietary models to validate task difficulty and identify limitations in existing search-agent benchmarks.
- Built an end-to-end evaluation framework and reproduced representative baselines, including **MMSearch-R1** and **DeepEyeV2**.
- Led the paper's **main experiments** and **key ablation studies**, covering benchmark validity and model behavior.
