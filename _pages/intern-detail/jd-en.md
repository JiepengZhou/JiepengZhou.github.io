## Key Workstreams

### JoyAI-Flash Evaluation

- Analyzed the **JoyAI-Flash** architecture and led the systematic evaluation of its general-purpose and web-search agent capabilities.
- Reproduced **LiveBench**, **BrowseComp**, and **HLE** to establish an end-to-end evaluation pipeline.
- Benchmarked representative models, including **MiniMAX**, **Qwen3.5**, and **DeepSeek-V3.2**, under controlled experimental settings.
- Studied multi-step reasoning, tool use, and the effects of inference parameters such as Pass@K, temperature, and Top-p.
- The evaluation results contributed directly to the publicly released **JoyFlash technical report**.

### Domain-Specific Customer-Service Model

- Led the complete development pipeline for a domain-specific model, including raw-data cleaning, **SFT/CPT data construction**, model training, deployment, evaluation, and analysis.
- Compared forward and reverse data-synthesis strategies and investigated the effect of data-mixture ratios on model performance.
- Iteratively improved the data-construction methodology based on controlled evaluations.
