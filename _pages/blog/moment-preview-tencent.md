## This week

A short Moment does not need to become a formal technical article. It can simply preserve the rhythm of a week, a new question, or a change in how you think about research.

## A safe way to write about work

Focus on personal growth, public knowledge, and general reflections. Avoid internal screenshots, unpublished results, private conversations, precise office access details, or anything covered by an NDA.
